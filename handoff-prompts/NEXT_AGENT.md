## 1. Product problem and success criterion

The merchant's job is to resolve an exception for a specific item in an order: understand the latest evidence, reconcile payment and fulfillment, determine a policy-backed customer obligation, identify what remains unknown, choose a safe next step, and verify its outcome. The system should complete the cognitive parts it can safely perform: gather relevant records, find contradictions, identify missing proof, classify the exception, decide the next nonfinancial case step, and prepare the operator's work. The judge must see verifiable product evidence of those actions, including whether a run is live or stored, rather than only an AI narrative.

Today the route structure spreads the story across a case Investigation page, a fixed-case Recovery Map, an Action Center, and a separate Demo Lab. F02–F05 fixtures vary mainly refund status around delivery failure (`scripts/db/seed-s1.ts`). Check these observations against current code before using them. A richer explanation alone will not meet this request: the next release must show AI completing a bounded investigation and a useful nonfinancial decision, while distinguishing model work, source evidence, deterministic policy, and actual payment outcome.

Define observable success before implementation.
